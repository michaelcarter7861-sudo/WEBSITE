ABOUT US

Clear calculations for better business decisions.

BusinessCalc Hub is an independent collection of practical calculators for small-business owners, ecommerce operators and people building their next idea.

Our goal is to make everyday business maths easier to use and easier to understand. Each tool is designed around clear inputs, transparent formulas and useful explanations.

Our approach

We aim to explain what each calculator measures, show the assumptions behind its result and avoid collecting information that is not needed to perform the calculation. Calculations run in your browser unless a page clearly states otherwise.

Results are estimates for general informational purposes and should not replace professional accounting, tax, legal or financial advice.
