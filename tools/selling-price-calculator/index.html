How to use the selling price calculator

Use a target-margin selling price calculator to work backwards from unit cost and the gross margin you want to achieve. Enter your total unit cost and target margin to estimate the required selling price.

The formula

Selling price = Unit cost ÷ (1 − Target margin ÷ 100)

Example and interpretation

Enter realistic figures from your own business records. Review each input before calculating, and compare the result with your accounting records. The result is only as reliable as the assumptions and values supplied.

Important considerations

A target margin is not the same as a markup. A 40% target margin means that gross profit should equal 40% of the final selling price, not 40% of the cost.

Depending on your business, you may also need to account for discounts, refunds, taxes, overhead, returns and other indirect costs. This calculator is a starting point, not a substitute for a complete financial model.
