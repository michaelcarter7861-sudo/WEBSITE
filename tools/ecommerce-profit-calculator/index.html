How to use the ecommerce profit calculator

An ecommerce order-profit calculator helps estimate how much remains from an order after common variable costs. Add your order revenue, product cost, shipping and fulfillment, marketplace fee, payment fee and advertising cost per order.

The formula

Order profit = Revenue − Product cost − Shipping − Marketplace fees − Payment fees − Advertising cost

Example and interpretation

Enter realistic figures from your own business records. Review each input before calculating, and compare the result with your accounting records. The result is only as reliable as the assumptions and values supplied.

Important considerations

This estimate excludes any costs you do not enter, such as returns, packaging, storage, discounts, sales tax, duties, overhead and subscription fees. Add those costs to your model separately where applicable.

Depending on your business, you may also need to account for discounts, refunds, taxes, overhead, returns and other indirect costs. This calculator is a starting point, not a substitute for a complete financial model.
