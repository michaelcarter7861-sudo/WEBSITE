How to use the break-even calculator

A break-even calculation estimates the sales volume at which total revenue covers fixed and variable costs. Enter your fixed costs, selling price per unit and variable cost per unit to estimate break-even units and revenue.

The formula

Break-even units = Fixed costs ÷ (Selling price per unit − Variable cost per unit), rounded up

Example and interpretation

Enter realistic figures from your own business records. Review each input before calculating, and compare the result with your accounting records. The result is only as reliable as the assumptions and values supplied.

Important considerations

Fixed costs generally stay stable over the relevant period, while variable costs change with the number of units sold. This simplified calculator assumes one product and a constant selling price and variable cost per unit.

Depending on your business, you may also need to account for discounts, refunds, taxes, overhead, returns and other indirect costs. This calculator is a starting point, not a substitute for a complete financial model.
