How to use the markup calculator

Markup measures how much you add to a product's cost to arrive at its selling price. Enter your unit cost and selling price to calculate markup percentage, gross profit and the equivalent profit margin.

The formula

Markup = (Selling price − Cost) ÷ Cost × 100

Example and interpretation

Enter realistic figures from your own business records. Review each input before calculating, and compare the result with your accounting records. The result is only as reliable as the assumptions and values supplied.

Important considerations

Markup is calculated using cost as the denominator. Profit margin uses selling price as the denominator. For example, a 50% markup on a cost of 20 produces a selling price of 30 and a 33.33% margin.

Depending on your business, you may also need to account for discounts, refunds, taxes, overhead, returns and other indirect costs. This calculator is a starting point, not a substitute for a complete financial model.
